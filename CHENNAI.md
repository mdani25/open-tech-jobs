## Tips for your profile (React + .NET + Python + Flutter)
- Search: `full stack developer`, `.NET developer`, `React developer`, `Flutter developer`, `software engineer I`, `graduate engineer trainee`.
- Aim at roles asking for 0-2 years. Roles asking for 3+ years are a stretch unless your skills match well.
- Put your live products (Puvi W Mart, dikshitech.com) at the top of applications, since these are your strongest proof.
