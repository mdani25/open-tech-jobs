The Global Shared Services (GSS) at Prime Video is an operationally-driven team of designers, producers, QA specialists and operations managers responsible for creating and delivering high volumes of digital assets. GSS is seeking a Visual Design Associate with the ability to deliver clean, digital-ready designs based on existing style guides and brand guidelines in a fast-paced production environment. The ability to manage multiple projects and large volume is essential.

Key job responsibilities
The candidate will have a comprehensive understanding of graphic design processes and software, specifically for graphic production, along with experience and a successful track record in digital marketing at fast-paced environments. Additionally, this candidate should have a keen eye for detail, and an excellent ability to communicate.

• Create on-brand digital design solutions that meet business goals and project requirements.
• Create best in class layouts and visuals that support and reinforce business objectives while partnering with business/marketing teams to ensure that designs meet brand and business needs.
• Ensure design requirements are met in adherence with campaign/project guidelines.
• Able to optimize digital images for web, mobile, big screen and other devices.
• Follow and apply graphic templates and style guides.
• Able to maintain quality on their own creative production
• Able to fulfil multiple projects requests while simultaneously meeting tight deadlines.

A day in the life
· The associate should be able to create innovative, multi-platform graphics based on the key art and other inputs provided by the partners/producers.
· The associate has to follow SOP/guidelines to complete the production task
· Able to partner with design leadership and product management partners to consistently deliver high-quality creative that meets placement specs.

Basic qualifications
- Knowledge of Adobe creative suite (Photoshop, InDesign, Illustrator)
- Bachelor's degree, or experience as technical specialist in design and architecture
- Experience working independently with minimal supervision
- Experience prioritizing and handling multiple assignments at any given time while maintaining commitment to deadlines, or experience with end-to-end project management
- Have an online portfolio demonstrating strong visual design skills and advertising experience across a range of media and platforms, along with work that reflects your design process
- Experience that includes strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and effective communication abilities, or experience working with customers with a passion for delivering exceptional service

Preferred qualifications
- Experience working as a producer, creative executive, or a similar role
- Experience working with global teams and diverse partners
- Experience in advertising
