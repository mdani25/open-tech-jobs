We are looking for a Media Asset Correction Associate to join our team, specializing in quality assessment and corrective editing of Prime Video audio, video, and subtitle assets. This role is responsible for ensuring that all playable media assets meet the required technical specifications and quality standards before they reach customers.
The ideal candidate will perform corrective editing using industry-standard tools, identify and resolve synchronization issues, maintain thorough documentation of quality findings, and escalate complex cases that require further investigation or redelivery from content partners. This position demands strong technical aptitude, meticulous attention to detail, and the ability to work methodically through high-volume asset queues in a production environment.

Key job responsibilities
- Perform quality assessments of Prime Video audio, video, and subtitle assets to ensure they meet required technical specifications and quality standards.
- Execute corrective editing of audio assets using appropriate editing tools, including trimming unwanted audio segments, adjusting timing and duration, and correcting audio anomalies.
- Align audio events with corresponding video content and correct audio-video synchronization issues.
- Identify and resolve subtitle timing, formatting, and synchronization discrepancies against audio and video references.
- Validate media assets against defined quality benchmarks and technical specifications (sample rate, bit depth, channels, timecode, duration).
- Maintain clear and thorough documentation of identified issues, corrective actions performed, and any remaining discrepancies for each asset.
- Escalate cases that cannot be resolved through standard editing procedures or require further investigation, clarification, or redelivery from content partners.
- Collaborate with content operations and partner management teams to ensure timely resolution of asset quality issues.
- Adhere to established workflows, standard operating procedures, and quality checklists throughout the correction process.
- Contribute to continuous improvement by identifying recurring quality patterns and suggesting process enhancements.

About the team
PV Publishing Ops is a dedicated operations team supporting Prime Video's growth worldwide. Our vision is to be the most customer-centric, agile, and efficient operations partner for PV, and our mission is to enable that growth through nimble, scalable processes until technology-based solutions are fully in place. We provide end-to-end execution support that focusses on On time Publishing and Quality ensuring content reaches customers quickly, accurately, and at scale.

Basic qualifications
- Basic understanding of audio editing and post-production concepts.
- Working knowledge of audio formats such as WAV, MP3, and AAC, including sample rate, bit depth, channels, audio duration, and timecode.
- Ability to identify audio-video synchronization issues and detect missing or extra audio events.
- Basic knowledge of audio waveforms, timeline editing, and sound-level adjustment.
- Understanding of subtitle file formats (SRT, VTT, TTML) and timing conventions.
- Familiarity with audio quality checks and reference-based validation techniques.
- Basic working knowledge of Adobe Premiere Pro or an equivalent audio/video editing application, including timeline navigation, waveform inspection, audio trimming, and alignment against video references.
- Familiarity with media players and tools used to inspect technical media properties (e.g., MediaInfo, VLC).
- Comfortable working with spreadsheets and documentation tools for issue tracking and reporting.
- Strong attention to detail with the ability to detect subtle audio and visual quality deviations.
- Good communication skills and ability to document findings clearly and accurately.
- Self-motivated with the ability to work independently and manage workload effectively.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience in audio correction, media quality control, video editing, or post-production workflows.
- Exposure to digital media delivery workflows, content validation pipelines, and asset management systems.
- Familiarity with multilingual audio tracks and descriptive audio (AD) tracks.
- Experience working with audio and video reference assets for quality comparison and validation.
- Knowledge of video codecs, container formats (MP4, MKV, MOV), and transcoding concepts.
- Experience with subtitle quality control, including timing validation and linguistic review coordination.
- Familiarity with content delivery standards for streaming platforms.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver consistently under tight timelines in a production environment.
